Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 2026  ·  myagentic-os.com

AgenticOS is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our Platform.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide Directly

Account registration information (name, email address, password) · Payment information processed securely by Stripe · Business information provided during onboarding · Task inputs and prompts submitted through the Execute tab · Communications you send to us

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

Log data (IP address, browser type, pages visited, time spent) · Device information (device type, operating system) · Usage data (features used, tasks run, session duration) · Cookies and similar tracking technologies

2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to provide, operate, and maintain the Platform; process payments and manage subscriptions; send administrative communications; respond to inquiries and provide customer support; improve and develop the Platform; detect and prevent fraud and security incidents; comply with legal obligations; and send marketing communications (you may opt out at any time).

3. How Your Data Is Processed — Anthropic API

Important: Task inputs submitted through AgenticOS are transmitted to Anthropic's Claude API for AI processing. By using the Platform, you consent to this transmission.

Your task inputs leave our servers and are sent to Anthropic for processing in accordance with Anthropic's Privacy Policy at anthropic.com/privacy.

We recommend you do not submit through the Platform: Social Security numbers, financial account numbers, Protected Health Information (HIPAA), attorney-client privileged communications, trade secrets, or personal information of minors.

4. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your information with:

Service Providers: Anthropic (AI processing), Stripe (payments), Vercel (hosting), and Clerk (authentication). These providers are contractually bound to protect your information.

Legal Requirements: When required by law, court order, or governmental authority.

Business Transfers: In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, with notice to you.

5. California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have the following rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act:

Right to KnowRequest information about the personal information we collect, use, and disclose.
Right to DeleteRequest deletion of your personal information, subject to certain exceptions.
Right to Opt-OutWe do not sell personal information. If this changes, you may opt out.
Non-DiscriminationWe will not discriminate against you for exercising your CCPA rights.

To exercise these rights, contact us at team.agenticos@gmail.com. We will respond within 45 days.

6. Data Retention

We retain your account information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services. Task input history is retained for 90 days unless you request earlier deletion. Payment records are retained as required by law. You may request deletion of your data at any time.

7. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including encryption in transit (SSL/TLS), secure cloud infrastructure through Vercel, and access controls. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of your information.

8. Children's Privacy

AgenticOS is not directed to children under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have inadvertently collected information from a child, contact us immediately.

9. International Users

AgenticOS is operated from the United States. If you access the Platform from outside the US, your information will be transferred to and processed in the United States. If you are located in the European Union, you may have additional rights under GDPR. Contact us to exercise these rights.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes via email or prominent notice on the Platform. Your continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.
